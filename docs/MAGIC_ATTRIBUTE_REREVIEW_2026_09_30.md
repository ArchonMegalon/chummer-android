# Saved Magic choices after an Attributes edit — 30 September 2026

This is a local Android repair and bounded native smoke, not a Play release,
new hosted seven-journey qualification or exhaustive SR5 parity claim.

## Product correction

An Attributes edit can make a previously saved Magic draft inexact even when
its choices and effective budgets remain legal. The inexact Magic budget now
opens a separate comparison once current Attributes are available. Core decides
whether the unchanged selection is admissible; the phone does not fabricate
ordinary editability or an exact budget.

The comparison keeps the original account/runner identity, lists the saved
choices, requires explicit confirmation and saves once. A pending save disables
its existing button and shows progress. Account transitions invalidate the old
review, including A → B → A. Known receipts survive refresh failure; uncertain
commit replies never authorize an automatic retry. English, German and Spanish
copy is included.
