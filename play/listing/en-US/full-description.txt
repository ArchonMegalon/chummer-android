Chummer is an experimental SR5 runner companion for phones, available to invited Internal testers.

CREATE AND CONTINUE YOUR RUNNER
Use native Creation and Career wizards. A minimal Priority route has been exercised from creation into Career, including save and reopen. Life Modules lets you review choices and cumulative rules effects. Other routes remain experimental; not every build method or Career action is complete.

YOUR ORIGIN STORY
Confirm your metatype, birth background and childhood before the opening story. Review generated text before accepting it and continuing to the next Life Modules choices. Read accepted chapters in the app and export them as an EPUB; reviewed scene illustrations are included offline when available. The confirmed starting situation appears before the story.

Generation requires a linked account, explicit consent to share character facts, an online service and available provider credits. It can be interrupted or unavailable. An entire accepted Origin book and the complete Life Modules-to-Career journey are still being completed. Audiobooks are not included.

WORK LOCAL-FIRST
Open and save runner files you choose through Android's document picker, without broad storage access. Account linking remains optional for local runner files. Connected features use encrypted connections to Chummer services.

CURRENT LIMITS
This is an Internal phone test, not a finished editor. Local checks cover selected routes; a physical Play-managed installation is not yet verified. Visible experimental features do not all have the same test coverage.
Full Editing, tablet or foldable support, SR4 or SR6 creation, Rook or live-avatar support, public availability, and production release are not included.

Chummer is a community character-management project. It is not an official tabletop game publisher app, does not include rulebook text, and does not replace the books required for play.
