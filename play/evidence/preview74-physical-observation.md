## Limits

existing-state restart observation. Gearhead is regression coverage; the phone's
six available choices do not cover all 65 new summaries. The broader copy set
has separate exact-input managed checks, not an exhaustive physical assertion.
Remaining Quality descriptions, rituals/enchantments, full-book quality and
all-method/Career polish remain open. Short original wording and passing checks
are not copyright clearance. No seven-journey, full beta, public, tablet, desktop,
Full Editing, audiobook or Rook completion claim.
